# ADR 0000: Decision Title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: Name/role
- Review date: YYYY-MM-DD or not applicable
- Related milestone: M1/M2/M3

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, affected modules, data sensitivity, and why a durable decision is needed.

## Decision

State the decision and its boundaries precisely.

## Alternatives Considered

List viable alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Document benefits, costs, operational impact, security/privacy impact, migration, rollback, and compatibility.

## Validation

List tests, metrics, experiments, or review evidence that can confirm or falsify the decision.

## Intentional Debt

If this decision accepts debt, include the risk, mitigation, owner, removal condition, and target milestone/date. Debt without these fields is not accepted.
